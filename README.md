# Day-O-Fathers
200+ lines of functional Shakespearean slop  

## SPL
This is written in Shakespeare language:
- Positive/Neutral Nouns = +1 eg. `King`
- Negative Nouns = -1 eg. `pig`
- Adjectives = *2 eg. `half-witted` or `charming`
Craft numbers out of combinations of nouns and adjectives then print the ASCII value.

## Long winded example
`Benvolio:
	You are the square root of the sum of the product of the sum of the square of the sum of 
	a half-witted smelly pig and an infected toad 
	and a snotty hog and the sum of the cube of the product of the sum of 
	a foul damned draught and a horrid leech 
	and a goat and the sum of the square of a hairy dirty death 
	and the product of a bad war and Hell and Microsoft.
	SPEAK YOUR MIND!`
  Translates to an expression of:
  sqr(((aa-n + a-n)^2 + a-n) * (((aa-n + a-n) * -n)^3 + (aa-n)^2 + (a-n * -n)) -n) = 89 = "Y"
